Design system

Cockpit design system

Material 3 light, one seed colour #3B6EA8 (HCT hue 256, chroma 42, tone 46), tonal-spot scheme. System font stack, vendored Material Symbols. Everything on this page is rendered from tokens.css + components.css + icons.svg; contrast ratios are computed in the page.

LiveBack-testMockPaperderivedComingprovenance tags: a texture or border style besides the colour (hatched, dashed, dotted)

Colour roles

Each swatch shows the role, its hex and the WCAG contrast of its "on" colour (or of the role on the surface, for accents). Text on a container needs 4.5:1; large text and icons 3:1.

Semantic: up / down / neutral / unclear, hit / miss

Never colour alone: an arrow, tick or cross plus the word. The up/down pair sits in the dataviz checker's CVD warn band (protan ΔE 6.8), which makes the glyph rule mandatory.

up down neutral unclear hit (inside the band) miss (outside) pending

Chart colours (dataviz skill, validated)

Series 1-4 in fixed order (adjacent pairs pass CVD ΔE ≥ 8 and normal-vision ≥ 15; series 2 is 2.83:1 on the surface, so it needs direct labels or the table view). Slots 1, 2 and 4 pass all-pairs (scatter, maps). Status up/down against series 1: all-pairs pass, up vs down in the warn band.

Light fill = 80% band, dark fill = 50% band (series 1 at two alphas), ink line = actual close, hit/miss dot with glyph, published range as an outlined box with its number.
