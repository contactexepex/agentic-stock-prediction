Track record · has it earned trust?

What this page proves. Every evening the system makes two kinds of promise for each company: a price range ("tomorrow the price stays between X and Y, 8 days out of 10") and, when it is at least 60% sure, a direction call ("70% sure it rises" = 7 times out of 10). Trust is earned by keeping those promises on real days, many times over, and by beating a coin and the costs. Four tests below say where it stands, what each means, what it takes to pass, and when that could happen. Until all four are green, every signal is paper: look, don't act.
