How to read this cockpitplain words, no jargon without a definition

What this is. A personal research tool that watches companies, in India and in the US. It collects news several times a day, captures prices before each market opens and after it closes, and runs its analysis at both points, so it knows what changed overnight and what changed during the session. For each company it works out the chance of a rise over the next day and the next five days, says YES or NO, and writes the result down so it can be checked later. A paper-trading book and further agents are being added to sharpen the forecasts; the Record page decides when any of it may be trusted. It never trades, and nothing here is advice. Until it has passed the four tests on the Record page, every signal is paper: something to watch, not to act on.

When can it be trusted with money? the four tests of the Record page

Not yet. The system has to pass all four tests below on real days, not only in the rehearsal on past prices. The Record page shows each test with today's number and the date it could turn green. Until then the signals are paper: follow them in your head, or in the pretend Portfolio, never with money.
