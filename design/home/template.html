How to read this screen

Banner: whether anything is worth doing today. "No proven strong signals" until a live YES exists and the track record proves the model; Paper candidates are companies at or above the call line without a confirmed call.
Market panels: the session, regime and the big numbers; yesterday's largest moves with a cause ( market-wide, company news, unexplained = mock, explainer agent coming); the week's events (eight weeks behind a click); what the latest run stored.
News: the last 24 hours of the stored archive. What moved the whole market (grouped stories, weighed by how many outlets reported them), the company stories that are confirmed or corroborated and so can carry a call, and what was set aside (single source, unverified, rumour, promotional) and why. Each company page has its own full news feed.
Colours: green up, red down, always with the sign; NO red, YES green (darker when strong); live stored by the pipeline, back-test rule replay, mock not built, paper unproven.
