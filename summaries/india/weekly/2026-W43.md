# India weekly summary, 2026-W43 (sessions 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-23; 2026-10-20 was a market holiday)

Written from the daily summaries of 2026-10-19, 2026-10-21, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23.

**Market:** the regime moved from TRENDING to CALM midweek. The Nifty 50 fell on every reported session: 1.11% on Oct 19, 0.34% on Oct 21 and 0.54% on Oct 22, closing at 22,687.65. India VIX rose from 14.005 to 15.76. Crude was the main pressure: Brent climbed from 107.97 to 114.42 and USD/INR stayed near 94.8. FIIs were net sellers on most days (5-day net sales of 8,431 crore on Oct 21, 6,103 crore on Oct 22); DIIs bought on most days.

**Sectors and stocks:**
- IT stayed strong: Nifty IT fell 1.28% on Oct 19, then rose 2.88% on Oct 21; TCS was up 19.09% over 20 days by Oct 22 (RSI in the mid-70s). INFY's Truist collaboration was confirmed by its exchange filing (nse-ann-106821932). INFY and DRREDDY reported Q2 results on Oct 23.
- Banks were weak after their Q2 results: HDFCBANK fell 2.31% on Oct 19 and again on an internal probe finding that senior executives leaked information (31517522a43fe707, corroborated); ICICIBANK fell 1.15% on Oct 19, then recovered.
- RELIANCE: the Jio Platforms IPO opened Oct 21 with strong demand (545039fb252f978d), but the stock fell 2.03% on Oct 22; results are due Oct 27.
- ULTRACEMCO fell 2.36% on its Q2 day, then rose after the results; LT won an airport order (nse-ann-106821513).

**Calls:** none all week. The forecaster abstained on every company each day: the signal model sat near its base rate, the open-to-close track record has 4 settled calls, all misses, and the collect gate blocked several companies each day. AI traders traded on paper only; on Oct 23 they made 17 paper predictions, all down.

**Ranges:** 1-day 80% coverage recovered from 12 of 20 (Oct 16 targets) to 19 of 20 on Oct 19 and Oct 21 targets.

**Data:** all collectors ran every day, but the collect gate failed every day on DUPLICATE_ID: old news items re-collected more than 9 days later were stored again under their old ids (issue #484 open). Recurring gaps: two old LT financials files return 404, NSE's pledge endpoint returns no rows, and results PDFs cannot be parsed yet. On Oct 22 and Oct 23 the news analyst scored the large backlog by keyword rules because of the time box.

**Lesson of the week:** with no model edge and a gate that keeps blocking companies, abstaining was right; fading the IT rally kept losing (the last TCS down call missed by 17.25%).
