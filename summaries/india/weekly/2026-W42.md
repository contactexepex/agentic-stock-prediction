# India weekly summary, 2026-W42 (sessions 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-16)

Written from the daily summaries of 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-16 and the 2026-10-19 pack.

**Market:** the regime moved from CALM to TRENDING by the end of the week. The Nifty 50 fell 0.7% on Oct 12, then rose 1.6% on Oct 13, fell 0.44% on Oct 14 and rose 1.66% on Oct 15 and 0.65% on Oct 16, closing at 23146.1504. India VIX ended the week near 14. **Calls:** three forecaster calls in the week, all TCS down (Oct 12 N+5; Oct 13 N+1 and N+5), each a small downward adjustment to the model for the ex-dividend date.
