# India monthly summary, 2026-10

Written from the weekly summaries 2026-W41 to 2026-W44 (no W40 summary exists; the first daily summary of the month is Oct 5) and the daily summaries of October.

**Market:** a choppy month that ended higher. The Nifty 50 fell to 22,231.8 by the Oct 8 close after the RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bp (794087886a3bc280), rallied in W42 to 23,146.15, slipped in W43 to 22,687.65 as Brent climbed above 114, and recovered in W44 to 23,053.10 on Oct 30. The regime moved between CALM, EVENT_HEAVY (RBI policy, monthly F&O expiry, FOMC) and TRENDING. India VIX stayed between about 14 and 17. Foreign investors sold heavily early in the month (FII -26,725 cr over the 4 reported days to Oct 8) and turned buyers in the last week; DIIs bought on most days.

**Themes:**
- IT was the month's strongest sector: TCS's Q2 results (nse-ann-106811186) started a rally that left TCS up over 20% over 20 days by late October and INFY up about 19%.
- Results season ran from TCS (Oct 8) through the banks (Oct 17), INFY and DRREDDY (Oct 23), LT, MARUTI and SBILIFE (Oct 27), ADANIPORTS and HINDUNILVR (Oct 28), INDIGO (Oct 29), and RELIANCE and SUNPHARMA (Oct 30). RELIANCE posted a record profit; MARUTI's profit fell 26% while its October sales hit a record.
- RELIANCE's Jio Platforms IPO opened Oct 21 and listed Oct 28.
- Metals and autos were weak and oversold for most of the month; DRREDDY ended it overbought after its results rally.

**Calls:** very few. The forecaster made TCS down calls on Oct 9 and Oct 13 (N+1 and N+5), fading the IT rally; all settled calls missed, the last by 17.25%. From Oct 19 the collect gate's duplicated news ids blocked most companies every day, and the signal model sat near its base rate, so the forecaster abstained on everything. The AI traders made paper calls only.

**Ranges:** 1-day 80% coverage was usually 17-19 of 20, with one bad day (12 of 20 on the Oct 16 targets) when a broad rally closed eight companies above their bands.

**Data:** collectors ran every day. Open problems: duplicated news ids re-stored after the 9-day de-duplication window (an open GitHub issue), NSE's pledge endpoint returning nothing, results PDFs that cannot be parsed, and news enrichment done by keyword rules on high-volume days.

**Lesson of the month:** fading a strong sector rally on one company's news lost every time; with no model edge, abstaining was right.
