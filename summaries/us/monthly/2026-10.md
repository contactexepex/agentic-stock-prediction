# US monthly summary, 2026-10

Written from the weekly summaries 2026-W41 to 2026-W44 (no W40 summary exists; the first daily summary of the month is Oct 7) and the daily summaries of October.

**Market:** a choppy, risk-off month for tech. The regime moved between TRENDING (Oct 7 and 8), CALM and EVENT_HEAVY (September CPI on 2026-10-14, FOMC on 2026-10-28). The VIX ran from 15.79 on Oct 7 to 16.13 on Oct 16, 19.57 on Oct 26 and 18.44 on Oct 30 (pre-open snapshots). The US 10-year yield stayed near 5% all month (5.269 on Oct 7, 4.989 on Oct 30). WTI rose from 89.87 to 104.1. After a hot CPI print, XLK fell 2.98% on 10-14 and Asia sold off on 10-15 (KOSPI -7.57%, Nikkei -4.81%). Defensives rotated in (XLV +2.53% and XLP +2.22% on 10-14); energy held up.

**Results season:**
- JPM (10-13) missed; BAC and PGR (10-14) fell 4.76% and 4.23%. BAC's $1.2 billion Treasury fine is corroborated (095cc8df62309ad2).
- GM and UAL (10-20): GM's EPS beat and $20 billion buyback did not stop a 6.84% fall the next day. META (10-21) printed EPS of 3.83 against a 5.46 consensus after a $9.9 billion legal charge, then announced a $95bn stock offering (cca7bea097221e03).
- GOOGL (10-28) fell 12.64% and CAT (10-29) fell 9.25%; LLY and MRK rose on their results. CVX and XOM reported 10-30.
- Airlines stayed weak most of the month (DAL cut its 2026 forecast on fuel costs).

**Calls:** only two, both NVDA up at N+5 (Oct 8, confidence 0.55; Oct 9, confidence 0.54). Both settled as misses, the first at -8.44%. From Oct 12 the forecaster abstained on everything: the signal model's P(up) sat within about a point of its base rate and few news events were verified. The weekly reviews found no model skill. The AI traders made paper calls: the pattern-mood trader on oversold names, the Sonnet combined trader following the model, the news-results and Opus combined traders mostly abstaining. Head-to-head picks were almost never viable at the owner's cost (2 viable on Oct 28 and 2 on Oct 30).

**Ranges:** 1-day 80% coverage was usually 15 to 19 of 20 (16 of 20 on Oct 15, 18 of 20 on Oct 16, 15 of 20 on Oct 23, 19 of 20 on Oct 28).

**Data quality (open problems):**
- The collect gate's DUPLICATE_ID: old news headlines stored again after the 9-day de-dup window (issue #484, first seen Oct 16). From Oct 21 it blocked calls on 9 tickers, growing to 17 of 20 by Oct 29 and 30.
- No results digests: SEC results rows were not stored, so no release was detected although many companies reported (new issue opened Oct 19, still open).
- SEC acceptance times unverified for some CIKs; Yahoo rate limits and failed option chains (Oct 22, 28, 29, 30).
- News enrichment often by headline rules on high-volume days. One JPM news row from a plain-http link blocked the gate on Oct 13.

**Lesson of the month:** with no model edge and verified news mostly already priced, abstaining was right; the one directional call lost.
