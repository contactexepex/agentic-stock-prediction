## Contract
- Routes (B7's table, `web/lib/ui/routes.ts`): `/{market}/strategy-lab` reads `strategies`, `/{market}/track-record`
  reads `track-record`; both through B7's `usePage` and `PageState` (loading, error with retry and fallback links).
- Strategy lab: the selected strategy follows the URL hash `#<strategy_id>` (B7's Help page and the portfolios page
  link it so); clicking a name writes the hash. The page opens on the payload's `default_horizon` (`all`), the
  accuracy view, the forward basis and market cost, as the mockup does.
- Forward and back-test rows are never pooled: every table, KPI, heatmap and line reads one basis; the cumulative
  lines and the week heatmaps exist for the forward basis only; the back-test basis shows the run's facts
  (`backtest_run`; a null `note` is left out).
- Track record: the weekly series (B13's top-level `weekly`, beyond the mockup) fills the "Accuracy over time" card
  for the selected basis key only (the mockup's empty state was the open data request B13 has answered).

Differences from the mockups, each on purpose:
- The luck verdict uses the Strategy lab's three words on every page (`edge` only when the corrected interval lies
  above zero, `loss` when wholly below, else `luck?`); the Rule vs AI and Paper portfolios mockups called any interval
  that excludes zero an edge, a loss included. With fewer than 20 trades an edge reads `edge? too few` (07's wording).
- A your-cost figure that is not stored shows as "not stored"; the 06 mockup put the market figure in its place.
- The topbar title and the company search are B7's shell.
