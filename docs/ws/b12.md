## Contract
- rm.stock, page_key = ticker of every collected company (ctx.collected; a deleted company never): schema
  `CompanyPage`, served as a 2.0 page (`cutoff`, `built_at`, `status.freshness` added by the route). The whole 03
  mockup page: PageHeader, HorizonChoice, status, reference_strategy, go_live, strategies (7 fields), companies,
  ticker, company, lifecycle, agreement, head_to_head, predictions, open_trades, trade_checks, settled, reasons,
  news, results, events, bars, on_company. Selection rules: design/mockups/03-company/notes.md, plus:
  - settled: the newest settlement of each trade (rm_common.settled_trades), every status, newest exit first;
  - trade_checks: the rows of the company's latest check with check_at (and computed_at) at or before the cut-off;
  - news: B11's news_items about the company first seen in the 30 days before the cut-off, status as of the
    company, newest 50 (`NEWS_DAYS`, `NEWS_MAX`);
  - events: B11's calendar_events from the session being predicted to 60 days later (`EVENT_DAYS`): the company's
    own rows, the market's major events and closed days;
  - bars: the last 250 split-adjusted sessions up to the as-of date (`BAR_SESSIONS`), `adjusted` = a split or bonus
    factor known by the cut-off was applied (the dashboard's BARS_SQL rule);
  - nested objects keep the keys the mockup names (enrichment incl. geopolitical, headline_history, reason_detail).
- Owner decision 2026-10-08 (relayed by the orchestrator): the company page's windows stay as above: news first seen
  in the last 30 days, newest 50; events for the next 60 days; the last 250 sessions of bars.
- rm.bars, page_key = ticker: `CompanyBars` {market, ticker, as_of, bars}, verbatim.
- rm.trades: page_key `_` = {market, as_of, open_trades (every open trade, B4's rm_common.open_trades), trade_checks
  (the market's latest check), settled (the trades that exited in the last 5 sessions to the as-of date, batch 2)};
  page_key = ticker = {market, ticker, as_of, open_trades, trade_checks, settled and reasons of the last 60 sessions
  (batch 2; batch 1 carried them all)}. Schema `TradesPage`, verbatim. `GET /api/v1/markets/{market}/trades[?ticker=]`.
- rm.lifecycle (batch 2), page_key `<ticker>:<session_date>` for every collected company and each of the last 30
  market sessions ending at the session being predicted: `LifecyclePage` {market, ticker, session_date, predictions
  (made for the session), head_to_head (picks of the session), trade_checks (every check of the session), settled
  (exit session = the day), reasons (session_date = the day)}, the 03 page's records and fields, verbatim. No mockup:
  the contract case checks its lists against the 03 mockup's. `GET .../stocks/{ticker}/lifecycle/{date}` (an older
  day or a malformed date: 404).
- rm.stock_strategies, page_key = ticker: `StockStrategiesPage` (04 mockup), served as a 2.0 page; strategies with
  description, compared_to, differs_in, parameters; on_company = scope strategy_company of the company, all horizons
  and per horizon; overall = scope strategy, all horizons (both accuracy view, from rm_common.lab_summary).
- Item schemas defined here: CompanyBar, StrategyPrediction, TradeCheck (its extra trade_check_rows columns optional,
  for B11's Home), SettledTrade, ResultsDigest, CompanyScoreRow, StrategyScoreRow. Referenced: B4's CompanyRecord,
  AgreementMap, OpenTrade; B13's HeadToHeadPick, PickCandidate, LuckTest, AiReason; B11's NewsItemRecord,
  CalendarEvent, LifecycleEventRecord.
- Functions for other pages: `rm_company.trade_checks(ctx)` (full trade_check_rows rows of the latest check, B11) and
  `rm_company.market_trade_checks(ctx)` (the TradeCheck fields, B13). api/openapi.yaml stays 2.0.0 (the major bump
  the owner approved covers the replaced payloads).
