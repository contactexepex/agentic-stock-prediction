# B13: Strategy and performance pages (Wave 3)

## Scope
The read models, API routes, web data layer, openapi paths and contract tests of four pages: 05-strategy-lab
(`GET /api/v1/markets/{market}/strategies`, `rm.strategies`), 06-rule-vs-ai (`/compare`, `rm.compare`),
07-paper-portfolios (`/portfolios`, `rm.portfolio`, reading B12's open trades and trade checks) and 08-track-record
(`/track-record`, `rm.track_record`; contract 2.0, owner decision 2026-10-08). Branch `build/b13-strategy-pages`.
Contract per page: `design/mockups/<page>/data.json` and `notes.md` (design/README.md, "Design closed").
Batch 1: 05, 06, 08 (on main, 64ccd56). Batch 2: per-strategy and per-company pages and rm.review for B5's read
tools, B2's stored back-test runs, the 07 page's write `POST /markets/{m}/paper-trades`, the removal of the planned
1.0 portfolio entries. Batch 3: the 07 read page (rm.portfolio), after B12's `rm_company.market_trade_checks`
reaches main.

## Contract
Every page: `market`, `name`, `currency`, `as_of`, `status`, `horizons`, `default_horizon`, `go_live` from B4's
`rm_common` (05 and 06 open on `default_horizon: "all"`; 08 keeps rm_common's N+1). `cutoff`, `built_at` and
`status.freshness` are added when served (serve mode `page`). Every read is as of the build's cut-off by each row's
own storage time, collected companies only. No build time inside a payload: B2's scoreboard rows carry the cut-off
as `as_of`; the pages show the data's as-of date there instead.

`track.as_of` is the data's as-of date (the newest indicator snapshot by the cut-off, as every page's header), not
the cut-off timestamp the catalogue's example shows: no build time sits in a payload (issue #234). `track.calls` and
`weekly` count every scored call, deleted companies' included: a call was made while its company was watched, and the
track record is the forecaster's record, not the watchlist's (issue #233).

The weekly series is a top-level key `weekly` (a ContractCase `extra_keys` entry), not `track.calls[].weekly` as
first proposed: the mockup shape check names extra keys at the top level only.

Times in the 06 records are ISO UTC with a Z. Records are ordered deterministically (rows by basis, view, scope,
strategy, pick rule, company, regime, horizon with `all` first; trades by entry date, company, pick rule, strategy;
picks by session, company, family, pick rule).
