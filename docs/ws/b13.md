## Contract
Every page: `market`, `name`, `currency`, `as_of`, `status`, `horizons`, `default_horizon`, `go_live` from B4's
`rm_common` (05 and 06 open on `default_horizon: "all"`; 08 keeps rm_common's N+1). `cutoff`, `built_at` and
`status.freshness` are added when served (serve mode `page`). Every read is as of the build's cut-off by each row's
own storage time, collected companies only. No build time inside a payload: B2's scoreboard rows carry the cut-off
as `as_of`; the pages show the data's as-of date there instead.
