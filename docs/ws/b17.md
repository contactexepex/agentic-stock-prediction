its row has a committer time before D's open (`lab/timing.first_commit_times`, `%cI`). A plain
`git pull --rebase` sets that time to the moment of the rebase. So 9e pushes right after the picks, and 9e and 12
rebase with `--committer-date-is-author-date`. Checked on git 2.43 in a scratch repo: a commit dated 2026-01-01,
rebased onto a newer upstream commit with that flag, kept `2026-01-01T00:00:00+00:00`.

### Schedule specs (sent to the orchestrator; the orchestrator creates the dispatcher triggers)
Every dispatcher fires into the orchestrator's session and calls `create_session` (claude-code-remote) with:
- `environment_id` env_0127KygCMEAV8xz4o8gXEd5S;
- `source_url` https://github.com/contactexepex/agentic-stock-prediction, `source_revision` main;
- `permission_mode` auto;
- tags `["market-brief", "<run tag>", "routine:<market>"]`.

This is the style of the existing daily and news dispatchers.

The pre-open (07:40 IST, 07:45 New York) and news dispatchers stay as they are.

Session models:
- intraday and post-close: Sonnet. They are light runs like the news run, and their agents (deviation-explainer,
  eod-analyst) set their own model in their frontmatter.
- weekly: Opus. The session merges a director's config-diff review once a week; the research-director agent is
  Opus by its frontmatter either way.

A trigger runs Monday to Friday. On an exchange holiday the intraday check writes only its `market_closed` row and
the post-close run stops at its session check.

The SPEC's 10:00 for the weekly run is on the hour. The trigger tool suggests moving such times a few minutes
earlier; that is the orchestrator's call.

## Open questions
1. **India close bar at 17:45 IST.** Yahoo still served India's 2026-10-08 daily bar with NaN OHLC at 20:46 UTC
   (`RELIANCE.NS` `history(period='5d')`), 10.5 h after the close. `collect_prices.py` then stored no 8 Oct bar
   and its NSE bhavcopy fallback filled nothing (`filled_from_nse` empty). B1 saw the same on 7 Oct.
   - Effect: a 17:45 IST post-close run would settle India's trades on a later run, flagged `exit_delayed`.
   - Default: keep 17:45 IST (SPEC section 7); B19 measures it on the first live runs.
   - Alternatives: move the India post-close later (for example 07:00 IST the next day, before the pre-open), or
     check why the NSE fallback does not cover a NaN Yahoo row.
2. **Late or mid-session pre-open.** 9c and 9d are skipped on a late or mid-session run: their inputs would not be
   the final close before D. Default taken; B2 asked to confirm.
3. **Warehouse sync count.** The new syncs are intraday x2, post-close and weekly per market. WS1 was asked whether
   `monthly_hours_ceiling: 7` still holds and whether to add kind labels. Default: the sync's ceiling skip (a
   logged, non-blocking skip) protects the budget.
4. **Inbox imports in the routines (ws4 #119).** Default: keep the direct imports (the readers de-duplicate by
   trade id) rather than dispatching `onboard.yml`.
