## Engine rules as built
- **Timing.** D = the first session whose open is after `made_at`. N+k exits at the close of the k-th session after
  D. A trade is settled only once the planned exit bar is final (close + `BAR_SETTLE_MINUTES`).
  - No open on D: `no_entry`.
  - No close on the exit session: settled on the next stored final close, flag `exit_delayed`.
- **Locked (F1.8).** Both the prediction and, for the head-to-head view, its pick must be made before D's open.
  - When the data root is a git repository, the commit that first added each row must be before the open too.
  - A row that no commit added is refused.
  - A row first seen in a shallow clone's boundary commit (listed in `.git/shallow`; its real first commit is cut
    off) is undated, so only its `made_at` is checked.
  - Refused ids are listed in the settle summary (`refused_not_locked`) and never settled.
  - `pick` refuses to run at or after D's open.
- **Amount.** Through `contracts.watchlist.trade_amount` (B1's accessor, merged): the `set_amount` override in force,
  which may raise or lower it (decision 44), else the default (₹1,00,000 / $1,000). The amount is copied into each
  prediction at `made_at`.
- **Quantity.** India `floor(amount / open)`; 0 gives `skipped_price_above_amount`. US `round(amount / open, 6)`.
- **Splits.** Adjustments with D < ex-date <= the exit: `exit_quantity = quantity / factor`, flag
  `split_in_window`. The target, range and price-reached measures use the window's bars on D's basis.
  - When the set of split records in the window changes later (a correction with `supersedes`), the next settle run
    writes a new row with `supersedes` and flag `resettled`.
  - Scoreboards and the strongest-strategy ranking use the newest settlement of each trade.
- **Costs** (`lab/costs.py`, owner decisions 50 and 52). Each line is rounded to cents and a view's total is the sum
  of its rounded lines.
  - **Market cost** (`paper_trades_settled.costs`, `net_pnl`, `return_pct`; strategies are ranked on it):
    - India: Axis brokerage 0.75% per side, at least ₹50 per order; STT 0.1% each side; exchange and SEBI fees;
      stamp duty 0.015% on the buy; GST 18% on brokerage + exchange + SEBI.
    - US: BUX's €0.99 per order at the EUR/USD close on or before each side's session; SEC fee; FINRA TAF.
  - **Your cost** (`cost_views`; the go-live bar and the owner's money use it) = market cost plus:
    - India: the NRI reporting charge ₹200 on the purchase date and ₹200 on the sale date, and the DP charge
      (provisional ₹30 or 0.04%).
    - US: the BUX Basic FX markup 0.75% of the value on the buy and on the sale, and the 0.20% a year portfolio fee
      on the entry value, pro-rated over the calendar days from D to the exit.
  - A trade whose fee cannot be converted yet (no EUR/USD stored at all) waits (`waiting_for_eurusd`).
- **Cost-viable flag** (decision 51, `lab/cost_views.py`).
  - When: at `pick` time, for every prediction of D that would trade (all families) and every pick.
  - How: the round trip of the company's amount bought and sold at C = base_close, in both views.
    `expected_move_pct = (target / C − 1) × 100`.
  - **Viable** (owner decision of 2026-10-07, made in session B6 for every session; it replaces decision 51's
    "move > cost"): `expected_gain_your_pct = p × move − (1 − p) × loss − your_cost_pct`, and
    `cost_viable = expected_gain_your_pct > 0`.
    - move and loss are the head-to-head picks' own (`lab/gain.py`), so Slack, the dashboard and the picks agree.
    - Without a probability or a range (always-up, momentum), `cost_viable` is null.
  - Stored as a `cost_views` row (`record_kind` prediction or pick), and on each head-to-head candidate
    (`expected_move_pct`, `your_cost_pct`, `cost_viable`).
  - Predictions are made and scored either way. A non-viable candidate can still be picked; the flag is shown
    (open question 9).
- **Automatic reason** (F1.10, `reasons.py`):
  - `market_pct` = beta (`beta_1y` as of the prediction, 1 when missing) x the benchmark's open-of-D-to-exit-close
    move.
  - `sector_pct` = the sector index or ETF move minus the benchmark move. Without an index: the other same-sector
    watchlist stocks' mean move minus the benchmark. Else 0.
  - `news_pct` = the rest (move − market − sector), credited only when verified news (confirmed_primary or
    corroborated as of settlement, timed from D's open to the exit close) exists and its summed sentiment has the
    rest's sign; else 0.
  - `company_pct` = what is left.
  - `reason_code` is the largest part by size; `reason_codes` add `target_reached` and `range_missed`.

## The two corrections to F1.7 (orchestrator decisions; proposed SPEC.md edit)
**1. Expected gain.** The spec's draft, `p × (target / C − 1) − (1 − p) × (1 − lo80 / C) − costs`, compares a
centre with a tail. On W1's example data, "best expected gain" picks N+1 for every group, always at a negative
value. A plain per-trade fix (`loss` = the expected shortfall below C) flips the bias: with the same p at every
horizon, the per-trade gain grows like √k, so N+5 always wins.

The built rule:
- From C and the strategy's own 80% range for the horizon, the exit close X is read as normal, with mean = target and
  sigma = (hi80 − lo80) / (2 × 1.2816).
- `move = E[X / C − 1 | X > C]` and `loss = E[1 − X / C | X < C]`, in % of C.
- `expected_gain = p × move − (1 − p) × loss − costs` (stored as `expected_gain_pct`; costs = the market-view
  round trip of the amount at C).
- "Best expected gain" = the horizon with the highest **expected gain per session held**, `expected_gain / k`
  (`gain_per_session_pct` in the candidates JSON). Ties: the shorter horizon.
- A prediction whose amount buys no whole share at C is no candidate (it would be skipped).
- **Every horizon shown** (owner decision of 2026-10-07, below). The pick's `candidates` JSON lists the strongest
  strategy's expected gain at every horizon it predicted for the company (the horizon list of config/strategies.yaml,
  so a horizon added later shows up without a code change). `eligible` marks the qualifying ones; only those can be
  picked.

Evidence, pasted from `python scripts/lab.py pick-study` (run with the owner-provided costs). The counts are of the
horizon picked per group (a group = one strategy, company and day). `per_trade` ranks the corrected gain per trade;
`draft` is the spec's formula. `candidates_positive` and `corrected_best_positive` count positive expected gains.

- **example**: W1's predictions; p rises with the horizon there.
- **india / us**: a back-test sample on stored bars (`pick_study.py`). For each watchlist ticker and each of its last
  120 as-of rows whose N+5 exit is stored, p, target and band come from the previous 250 rows' N+k outcomes (no
  look-ahead); horizons with p >= 0.55 are candidates.
- The corrected rule picks several horizons on all three samples, and all five in the US. The draft never picks a
  positive gain on the example and India samples.
- **India bias.** With the owner-provided Axis costs (about 2% per round trip in the market view), none of the 1419
  India candidates has a positive expected gain. When every gain is negative, dividing by k shrinks the long
  horizons' losses most, so the India picks lean to N+5 (380 of 478). In the round-1 run, at lower provisional
  costs, some India gains were positive. This bias is recorded here and in open question 1.

**2. Strongest strategy** (decision 41).
- Strategies of the family with at least 20 settled accuracy trades on the company rank first, by profit after
  costs on it.
- The rest follow by profit after costs on all the market's companies.
- **Strategies with no settled trade rank last**, so a strategy with no record is never "strongest" ahead of one
  with a record.
- Ties: more settled trades, then the lower id.
- A re-settled trade counts once (its newest row).
- Tested in `test_lab_picks.py`.

## F2 strategies as built
- **Probability of the model signal:** `p = sigmoid(logit(prob_model) + news_weight × coefficient × clip(sum of item
  weights, ±score_cap))`.
  - The item weights are the signal model's (`model/news_score.py`), with only the strategy's `news_statuses` and
    `news_materiality` kept. Rumour, promotional, unverified and contradicted items always weigh 0.
  - The reference reproduces the score's `prob_up`; news weight 0 is model-only.
- **Evidence.** The model score id and the feature snapshot id first, then the news ids counted. News ids are cited
  only when the first (after sorting confirmed, corroborated, single-source) is verified (DESIGN.md 3b).
- **Blocks.** BLOCKED quality or `days_to_earnings <= 1`: an abstention row per strategy (`blocked_quality`,
  `earnings_window`).
- **Regime filter.** Written with `qualifies: false` in UNSTABLE / EVENT_HEAVY (W1 default 5).
- **Cross-market strategy** (`rule.model_news_global.v1`): abstains (`abstained`, "no cross-market model score")
  until B10 supplies a cross-market model variant.
- **Momentum.** Compares the as-of close with the previous close put on the same split basis.
- **Target and range.** The B10 range centre and its bands (never narrowed).
- **Live inputs.** `predict` reads `contracts.horizons.scores_asof` / `ranges_asof`. Until B10 builds them it
  refuses with a message and writes nothing. The tests use W1's example predictions as the per-horizon score and
  range fixtures.

**Back-test** (`backtest`, F2.3).
- Always-up and momentum on adjusted bars stored by the clock, plus the history cache with `--history`.
- Basis `backtest`, written only to `--out`, never to data/.
- Both cost views come from `lab/costs.py`; net profit and return are in the market view, and `your_cost` repeats
  them.
- Model-only needs B10's per-horizon walk-forward probabilities and is not run yet.
- No EURUSD bars are stored yet, so the US order fee uses an ASSUMED constant 1.17, labelled in the output.

## Scoreboard (F7) as built
- `scoreboard.py` rows per market x view x basis, with `scope`:
  - `strategy` ("all" and each horizon);
  - `strategy_company` ("all" and each horizon);
  - `pick_rule` (head-to-head, "all" and each horizon);
  - `strategy_regime` (new: F2.6 asks for every strategy per regime).
- Each row (market-cost view, the ranking):
  - trades, net_pnl, mean_return_pct, win_rate;
  - target_reached_rate and median_reached_session (over trades with a target);
  - avg_target_error_pct, range_hit_rate, worst_losing_streak, max_drawdown, sample_badge;
  - the luck test.
- `your_cost` (owner decision 50) repeats net_pnl, mean_return_pct, win_rate, worst_losing_streak, max_drawdown and
  the luck test on `return_pct_your` from the `cost_views` settlement rows.
- **Luck test** (`luck.py`): percentile bootstrap (2000 resamples, seed from the slice key) of the mean net return %.
  - Corrected interval: Bonferroni over the m rows compared in the same scope, market, view, basis, company, regime
    and horizon.
  - `corrected` is true only when the corrected interval excludes zero.
- **Go-live** (F7.2, on the your-cost view; `cost_view` names the view used, market only while a your-cost row is
  missing; the spec's proposals until the owner sets them):
  - at least 2 months and 300 trades (accuracy view, forward);
  - beats the best baseline's net profit with `corrected` true;
  - max drawdown within 10 x the default amount;
  - net profit positive in both calm and volatile (UNSTABLE / EVENT_HEAVY) regimes.
- **Comparisons** (`compare.py`), each with pairs, net profit of each side, wins and the mean difference with a
  bootstrap interval:
  - rule vs AI on identical company-days per pick rule;
  - gain-pick vs probability-pick per family;
  - each strategy vs its `compared_to` on identical predictions.
- **Heatmaps** (`heatmaps.py`): win rate and net profit by strategy x horizon, x company and x reason code, per ISO
  week and "all"; cumulative profit lines per strategy (accuracy) and per family:pick rule (head-to-head).

## News-impact study (F3) as built
`news_impact.py` and `reports.write_news_impact`:
- Events: enriched news on its primary tickers, first seen and enriched by the run time, status as of then. One
  event per company and same-event cluster.
- D is the first session whose open is after the item's time. Only windows whose exit bar is final count.
- `abnormal = stock − beta × benchmark − (sector index − benchmark)` (sector part 0 without an index).
- Mean and a normal 95% interval; `enough` from 10 events. Below that, no mean is stored ("not enough events yet").
- The planted-effect fixture is recovered exactly (`test_news_impact_recovers_a_planted_effect`).

## Horizon changes in portfolio/ (W1 file list)
- `proof.py`: cells for every horizon of config/strategies.yaml. A forecaster call counts for N+k only when its
  `horizon_label` is `n_plus_k`, or when it is unlabelled 1-day open-to-close (= N+1). The legacy 5-day calls (exit
  D+4) are never pooled with N+5.
- `signals.py`: Hold rows for every active ticker x horizon. A legacy 5-day score gets no proof cell.
