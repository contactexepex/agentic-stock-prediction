## Contract
- `config/costs.yaml` `broker:` values are final for paper trading (SPEC decision 53). No consumer change.
- ai_replay `prepare` cutoff: the routine's start on the session after D, at India 07:40 IST / US 07:45 New York, for
  every as-of date. Before 2026-10-07 the routine started 30 minutes later, so a replay's cutoff is earlier than that
  day's real start and sees less data, never more. The cutoff is still capped at 1 minute before the open.
- `config/markets/<market>.yaml` `company_meta:`: per-company metadata (name, yahoo, aliases, queries, news_names,
  news_exclude, wire_names, broker, adr, nse ...). An entry never adds a company to a seeded market. Membership is
  B1's watchlist events (`load_market` rebuilds `cfg["tickers"]` from them; `watchlist(market, as_of, state)`).
  Every reader of `cfg["tickers"]` keeps working unchanged: it already read the loader's rebuilt list, which equals
  the events. The ticker lists under `sectors:` stay (sector order; the sectors of an unseeded root).
