## Contract

### Contract layout (api/)
- `api/openapi.yaml` (B4): shared parts and platform paths. `api/paths/<page>.yaml`: a map path -> path item.
  `api/schemas/<page>.yaml`: a map schema name -> schema. Every `$ref` is written against the bundled document
  (`#/components/schemas/X`). `marketbrief.warehouse.openapi_spec.bundle()` merges them; a name defined twice fails.
- Shared components a page reuses: `ReadModelMeta` (envelope), `PageHeader` (market, name, currency, as_of, and the
  serve-time cutoff and built_at), `HorizonChoice`, `StatusBlock`, `GoLive`, `StrategyEntry`/`StrategyMap`, `Market`,
  `Ticker`, `Horizon`, `IsoDate`, `IsoTime`, `NullableNumber`, `NewsStatus`, `LabelBasis`, `Problem`, the parameters
  `Market`, `Ticker`, `IdempotencyKey` and the responses `NotFound`, `Unavailable`, `NotImplemented`, `Conflict`,
  `Invalid`, `Unauthorized`.
- A read operation: `x-read-model: rm.<table>` (a registered builder's table), 200 = `allOf [ReadModelMeta,
  {payload: <PagePayload>}]`, plus 404 and 503. A write: an `Idempotency-Key` parameter.

### Read-model framework (scripts/marketbrief/warehouse/)
- A page module `rm_<page>.py` declares `BUILDERS = (PageBuilder(table, schema, build, owner=..., serve="page"),)`
  and `CONTRACT_CASES = (ContractCase(...),)`. The registry finds every `rm_*` module (no list to edit); a table built
  by two modules is an error.
- `build(ctx)` returns `{page_key: payload}`. `ctx` (`BuildContext`): `cfg`, `con` (the repo's DuckDB),
  `cutoff_time`/`cutoff` (the run's clock, MB_NOW-aware), `dashboard` (gather_dashboard as of the cut-off, computed
  once), `as_of`, `companies` / `collected` / `active` (B1's `watchlist(market, cutoff, "collected")`; deleted
  companies never appear), and `shared(name, compute)` (computed once per build).
- Shared blocks (`rm_common.py`, one per catalogue record every page repeats): `header`, `horizons`, `status_block`,
  `strategies(ctx, fields)`, `go_live`, `lab_summary` (B2's scoreboard, comparisons and heatmaps of the collected
  companies' settled trades as of the cut-off) and `settled_trades`; batch 2 adds the shared derived records of
  `rm_entities.py` (re-exported by `rm_common`):
  - `agreement(ctx)` -> {"1".."5": AgreementRow list by rank}: the active companies, from the newest strategy
    predictions made by the cut-off (each id's first stored row);
  - `open_trades(ctx)` -> OpenTrade list by trade id: B9's `intraday/trades.open_trades` as of the cut-off (qualifying
    predictions and picked head-to-head picks made before D's open, no settlement row, at most
    `trades.max_sessions_past_exit` sessions past the exit), entered (D's open stored) with at least one share
    (F1.4), valued at the newest stored close (sessions up to the as-of date, collected by the cut-off) on today's
    price basis: the entry times the split/bonus factors after D, the target times those after the as-of date (as
    intraday/trade_rows.py does);
  - `companies(ctx)` -> CompanyRecord list, active first: B1's accessor, the newest two raw closes, `agreement_n1`
    (active only) and the count of `open_trades`.
- Rules: read rows stored at or before `ctx.cutoff` only; filter stored rows on `ctx.collected` (and `ctx.active`
  where the page shows active companies only); never put a build time into a payload.
- Serve modes: `page` (2.0 page payloads: the route adds `cutoff` and `built_at` at the top and `freshness` inside `status`),
  `status` (adds `freshness` at the top), `verbatim` (1.0). Freshness: stale after 480 minutes.
- The sync validates every payload against its schema before writing; an invalid page keeps its old row and fails the
  build (`invalid_pages`). After the commit a MotherDuck sync posts the changed and deleted keys to
  `/api/v1/internal/revalidate` (REVALIDATE_SECRET, VERCEL_AUTOMATION_BYPASS_SECRET; non-blocking, summary key
  `revalidate`).

## Go-live: live strategies only (B19's request, 2026-10-08)
From Wave 5's switch a strategy trades for real only from its `live_from` (B2's `lab/registry.is_live(strategy, D)`);
rows made before (rehearsal runs) are never shown or counted. Under the owner's ownership rule each page owner
filters the rows its own modules read, and B4 its shared records; it lands in steps so main stays green:
1. B4 step 1: `rm_entities.live_rows(rows, day_key)` (re-exported by `rm_common`) keeps a row whose strategy is live
   on its D (`session_date` of a prediction or pick, `entry_date` of a trade or check) and drops rows without a day.
   A pick without a strategy (status `no_candidate`) is kept when some strategy of its family is live on its D, else
   dropped (a rehearsal pick run). `tests/conftest.py` `all_strategies_live` (module fixture) makes every strategy
   live for page tests built from W1's catalogue rows. No behaviour changes in this step.
2. B11 (`rm_home` picks, `rm_watchlist` ranges), B12 (`company_sources.read_sources`: predictions, picks, checks)
   and B13 (`rm_compare.picks`) apply `live_rows` in their own commits and request the fixture in their page tests.
3. B4 step 2: `agreement` reads the predictions with `session_date` on or after the registry's earliest
   `live_from` (none while no strategy is live), keeps `live_rows` and takes the newest batch of those (a newer
   rehearsal batch never hides the live one); `open_trades` keeps `live_rows` on `entry_date`, so the `companies`
   counts follow; `settled_trades` applies B2's `lab/scoreboard.live_settlements`, so `lab_summary`, `go_live` and
   the `strategies` settled counts see live trades only; `strategies.live` is `registry.is_live` on the session.
   Tests in `tests/test_rm_entities.py`: nothing live, a newer rehearsal batch, a strategy not live losing its open
   trade and settlements. It merges after B12's filter commit, whose page tests then request the live fixture.
