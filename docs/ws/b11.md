## Contract
Everything is read as of the cut-off (the run's clock, MB_NOW-aware): nothing stored, scored, verified, fetched or
seen after it reaches a record.

- `news_items(...) -> list[dict]`: one catalogue "News item" per item first seen in (since, cutoff] (since None: all
  stored), newest first then by id, uncapped. An item is listed when the news analyst scored it by the cut-off
  (`news_enriched.analyzed_at`) and it is about a company collected at the cut-off (its first primary ticker; B1's
  records as of the cut-off) or is market-wide with relevance >= 0.4 (W1's rule of the catalogue build). A company
  deleted by the cut-off is never shown (decision 12): it is taken out of `tickers` and `primary_tickers`, and an
  item tagged only with deleted companies, or whose primary companies are all deleted, is left out (a deleted
  `status_ticker` gives no items). `tickers` keeps the items tagged with one of them (B12's
  per-company filter). Fields: `id, market, tickers, primary_tickers, title, source, source_domain, url,
  published_at, first_seen_at, enrichment{event_type, materiality, sentiment, relevance, novelty, urgency,
  priced_in, geopolitical, analyzed_at}, status, status_as_of, cluster_id, independent_origins, primary_ids,
  headline_history[{seen_at, title}], headline_history_status, scope, category, feed, summary, summary_source,
  market_moving, origin` (`stored`). `status` is the item's own status for its first primary ticker
  (`news_status_ids_asof`), or for `status_ticker` when given (the company page's own company, B12), empty for a
  market-wide item without one; `independent_origins` and `primary_ids` come from the
  newest cluster row of `news_verified_asof`; `summary` / `summary_source` and `market_moving` follow W1's rules
  (`design/catalogue/catalogue_newsfeed.py`).
- `news_window(...) -> (items, window)`: the News page's and Home's selection: `news_items` of the `days` before the
  cut-off, capped at `max_items` by `capped` (half the cap for company items and half for market-wide ones, each
  ordered market movers, then high materiality, then newest; a share one scope leaves unused goes to the other),
  newest first. `window` = `{days, from, to, max_items, stored_in_window, older_hidden}`.
- `calendar_events(...) -> list[dict]`: catalogue "Calendar event" rows from `from_date` to `to_date`: the market's
  events of `config/events.yaml` (`core/calendar.market_events`), the weekdays the exchange is closed, and the
  results and ex-dividend dates of `tickers` (None: every company collected at the cut-off, active and inactive, for
  B12's pages of inactive companies; the market pages pass the active ones), from the stored `events` read with the
  dashboard's `COMPANY_EVENTS_ASOF_SQL`: per company and type, the newest date first seen by the cut-off;
  `reaction_sessions` from `earnings_reaction.affected_sessions`. Order: date, major first, market rows before
  company rows, type, name.
- `lifecycle_rows(con, cutoff, shown)`: the `watchlist_events` rows recorded by the cut-off of the tickers shown,
  newest first, with the mockup's fields (times ISO UTC); since batch 2 a company deleted by the cut-off named in a
  `reason` is replaced by "(deleted company)" (case-sensitive whole word, so "all" is not ALL).
  `command_rows(con, cutoff, deleted)`: the `command_log` rows of the five company tools received by the cut-off,
  newest first, `arguments` reduced to the mockup's keys (`market`, `symbol`, `ticker`, whichever the command has;
  `reason` and the like never reach the page). A command about a deleted company (its `ticker` or `symbol` argument,
  or a record id it wrote) has its ticker argument, message, idempotency key and record ids masked (decision 12);
  any other command keeps its fields, with a deleted ticker named in its message replaced by "(deleted company)". `shown_companies(market, cutoff)`: B1's records
  without the deleted ones, and the deleted tickers. `inactive_news(items, companies)`: items tagged with an
  inactive company and first seen since it went inactive.
