## Contract
**Trader record** (what an agent writes, `work/traders/<strategy_id>.jsonl`): `strategy_id`, `ticker`,
`horizon_days` (1, 3 or 5), `direction`, `prob_up`, `target_price`, optional `range_widen`, `evidence_ids` (1-3),
`reason` (at most 60 words), `prompt_version`. The combined traders also write `model_prob`,
`agent_adjustment` and `adjustment_reason`. A derived field may be copied, but it must then equal the stored value.
An abstain line is `{"strategy_id", "ticker", "abstain": true, "horizons", "reason", "prompt_version"}`.
`made_at`: the three Sonnet traders have no clock (no Bash) and write none; the gate stamps every line of theirs with
the file's write time, capped at the gate's clock, and replaces a made_at they state (warning MADE_AT_STAMPED). The
forecaster (Bash) writes its own, and the stamp only fills lines without one.
Every other column of `strategy_predictions` is derived from stored data:
- id, as_of_date, session_date, exit_date;
- base_close, the range edges and range_id;
- confidence, threshold, qualifies;
- regime, quality, amount, currency;
- config_hash (`sha256:` + 16 hex of the canonical JSON of the registry entry);
- method_version `traders-v1`.

**Gate** (`gate.py`, `gate_evidence.py`; codes in `constants.py`). It is shared with the existing rules:
`prediction_rules.check_news_status` (DESIGN.md 3b) and `model.forecast_rules.anchor_errors` (forecast-v11) are called
unchanged. Each rule and its code:
- TRADER_SCHEMA: unknown or missing fields.
- TRADER_STRATEGY: the record must carry the trader's own strategy_id, its prompt_version and one of its horizons.
- TRADER_TICKER: the company must be active.
- QUALITY_BLOCKED, EARNINGS_WINDOW: no call with quality BLOCKED, with earnings within 1 day, or with no snapshot or
  regime row.
- TRADER_DUPLICATE: the same id twice in one file is refused, and so is a prediction and an abstain line for the
  same company and horizon, in either order. An abstain line's made_at may not be after the gate's clock. An id already stored is skipped with an ALREADY_STORED
  warning, so a rerun stores nothing twice.
- TRADER_TIME: made_at must be ISO UTC, not after the gate's clock (5 minutes of tolerance) and not after the deadline.
- RANGE_NARROWED: the published range of the horizon and D must exist. It may only be widened (range_widen 0-0.5,
  the same formula as `range_row.py`). Band edges written by the trader must not be narrower than the published ones.
- NUMBERS_MISMATCH: a copied derived field must equal the stored value.
- TARGET_PRICE: the target must lie inside the widened 80% range and on the call's side of C.
- TRADER_PROBABILITY:
  - direction is the side of 0.5 that prob_up is on (0.5 is refused);
  - confidence = max(p, 1 - p) must be within 0.50-0.90;
  - in an UNSTABLE regime, confidence may be at most 0.65.
- TRACK_RECORD_BAND: a band with at least 20 settled trades whose hit rate is below the band's lower edge refuses any
  confidence inside it.
- EVIDENCE_UNKNOWN, LOOK_AHEAD:
  - news, filing and announcement ids must be stored and public by made_at;
  - input ids `features:<as_of>-<ticker>`, `regime:<as_of>` and `model_scores:<as_of>-<ticker>-<k>d` must name this
    company and day;
  - the range must have been published, and the model score computed, by made_at: `load_inputs` reads B10's
    `ranges_asof` and `scores_asof` as of each record's made_at, so a later rescore is never used;
  - a cited `features:` or `regime:` id must have been computed by made_at.
- BLIND_INPUT: a trader may cite only the kinds of its inputs. The news and pattern traders may not state the anchor
  fields.
- NEWS_STATUS_*: the first cited news id must be confirmed_primary or corroborated. Rumour and promotional ids are never
  allowed; a contradicted id only with range_widen > 0. A single_source or unverified id caps confidence at 0.85.
  When no status row exists yet, NEWS_STATUS_MISSING is a warning.
- MODEL_ADJUSTMENT (combined traders only):
  - model_prob must equal the stored score;
  - |adjustment| <= 0.10, with a reason;
  - prob_up = model_prob + adjustment;
  - a non-zero adjustment must cite a news, filing or announcement id.
  Without a stored score, the anchor fields must stay out (MODEL_SCORE_MISSING warning).
- TRADER_REASON: the reason must have 1-60 words.

**Add** (`outcome.py`):
- Kill switch off: every active company gets `killed`.
- Gate clock past the deadline: `timeout` for every company without a stored prediction; nothing from the file is
  stored.
- Attempt 1 with any error: nothing is stored (exit 1). The caller sends the errors back once.
- Attempt 2, or a file without errors: the valid predictions are stored. Then one abstention row per trader, company
  and day, with the strongest code: killed > timeout > blocked_quality > earnings_window > gate_failed > abstained. It
  stores `horizons`, `gate_codes` and `attempts`.
- A company with no line at all gets `gate_failed` / NO_RECORD, never a missing row.

**Trader input** (`prepare.py`): the frame (as-of date, D, deadline, exit dates, regime) and the companies table (call
allowed or not, C, the published range of each horizon; for the combined traders also the model's P(up)). Then the
trader's own track record and the context-pack sections its `inputs` allow (`SECTION_INPUTS`). Sections no category
names are left out for every trader: open predictions, the forecaster's track record and lessons, range scorecards,
judge FAILs. The budget's `max_input_kb` cuts later sections and lists them.

**Post-close**:
- Settling is B2's engine: `python scripts/lab.py settle` (step 4 of `routine/POSTCLOSE_PROMPT.md`). It settles both
  views once, refuses predictions or picks not locked before D's open, re-settles on a split correction and writes
  `cost_views`.
- The EOD facts quote the market-cost view only (`paper_trades_settled` costs, net_pnl, return_pct), the view
  strategies are ranked on; the your-cost view in `cost_views` is not quoted (owner decision, 2026-10-07).
- `eod-prepare`: the results per family (accuracy view) and per pick rule (head-to-head view). Items to explain: every
  head-to-head trade settled today, then the 5 biggest wins and 5 biggest misses of the accuracy view by return_pct
  (ties: trade id).
- `eod-validate` / `eod-add`:
  - every item explained exactly once with its id and kind;
  - reasons of 1-60 words, the summary 1-150;
  - cited ids: the trade id first, then only its news ids;
  - every id in the text is cited;
  - every number is a stored fact, with signed percentages carrying the right sign;
  - no advice or forecast words.
  `--valid-only` stores the valid reasons and the deterministic results (summary null = withheld). A day with no
  settled trade stores the fixed line "No paper trades settled on <date>."

**Weekly** (`director-*`):
- Inputs: leaders per family to date and this week (accuracy view, after costs), the week's news_impact, eod_analyses,
  trade_reasons_ai and lessons, the registry, and the current text of `config/strategies.yaml`, `model.yaml`,
  `ranges.yaml` and `costs.yaml`.
- The gate checks:
  - findings and rationales cite input ids, every number comes from a cited record, and there is no trade advice;
  - proposal ids are `p-<week>-<n>`, the kind is from its enum and the file is allowed;
  - the diff applies to a copy of the current file (`git apply` in a temporary folder, outside any repository);
  - a live strategy may never change; a change needs a new id.
- Nothing is applied. `director-add` writes `reports/<market>/research-<week>.md` and one `research_reviews` row with
  every proposal `proposed`.

## Open questions
Each question has the default B3 took.

1. **Trader entry point.** B3's row owns no `scripts/<name>.py`, so the command is
   `PYTHONPATH=scripts python -m marketbrief.traders`. Default: keep it. Wave 5 may add a thin `scripts/traders.py`
   for the usual `python scripts/...` form.
2. **Settlement step.** Owner decision (2026-10-07): use B2's engine. Step 4 of `routine/POSTCLOSE_PROMPT.md` runs
   `python scripts/lab.py settle`; B3's own loop and its `settle` command are removed.
3. **Ranges the traders copy.** Owner decision (2026-10-07): the traders' N+k ranges carry no forecaster drift, so
   each trader is independent. This is a request to B10 (the ranges are its work): publish the N+k ranges the gate
   reads through `ranges_asof` without the AI call's centre shift and widening. Until B10 confirms, the gate takes
   whatever `ranges_asof` returns.
4. **Track record source.** A trader's band record comes from its settled accuracy-view trades, a hit being gross
   P&L > 0. Down calls do not trade, so they are not in it. Default: up calls only, until B2 or B10 store a direction
   score for strategy predictions.
5. **Opus trader fairness.** The Opus trader runs as a separate forecaster invocation reading only the same input file
   as combined Sonnet. It does not see the bull and bear cases. Default: yes, so the comparison tests only the model.
6. **Biggest wins and misses.** These are ranked by return_pct (decision 44: amounts differ per company) on the
   accuracy view. Default: as built.
7. **config_hash.** It is `sha256:` + 16 hex of the canonical JSON of the registry entry. B2 should hash rule
   strategies the same way. Default: B3's definition; B2 aligns or the orchestrator picks one.
8. **Budget.** `max_minutes` is a caller-side limit; the hard limit is the deadline. `max_input_kb` is enforced by
   `prepare`. Default: as built; Wave 5 measures the run's duration.
9. **EOD costs.** Owner decision (2026-10-07): the EOD reasons and summary quote the market-cost view only.
